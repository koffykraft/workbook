Coffee, from farm to cup.

Free tools for growers, roasters and brewers. Keep records of your plots, harvests, processing and roasts, brew and cup better, and follow every coffee back to where it grew.

No account needed. Works on your phone, even without signal. Made in Kerala, with Indian coffee and Robusta built in.
