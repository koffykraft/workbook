What This Is

KoffyKraft is a tool built by an individual roaster. It uses cloud storage to sync your roasts across devices for convenience. This is temporary storage. Not a backup service. Not permanent archive.

Your Responsibility

1. You own your roast data. You maintain full ownership of everything you input.
2. You are responsible for backups. Develop a habit of saving to your own device regularly.
3. Export monthly to Dropbox, Google Drive, or your computer. This should be as routine as backing up any important file.
4. Online data is not 100% secure. No online service guarantees absolute security. Treat this like any other online storage.
5. You keep your sign-in code private. Treat it like a password.

Storage Limits

1,000 roasts per account. 50 MB total storage. Roast files accumulate quickly. Like any working tool, clean up old data regularly.

We will not access, read, or use your roast data. We will not share or sell your data. We will not track you. We do not collect analytics.
