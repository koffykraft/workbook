Privacy Policy

What We Collect

Your email address (for sign-in only). Your roast records (dates, temperatures, notes). Authentication tokens (stored in your browser).

What We Don't Collect

Your location. Device information. Analytics or tracking. Photos, videos, or audio files. Payment information.

Data Storage

Your roast data is stored in Cloudflare D1, a private encrypted database. Your authentication token is stored in your browser's localStorage.

Backups

You are responsible for maintaining your own backups. We recommend exporting your roastbook monthly. KoffyKraft is not a backup service.

Deletion

You can export all your data and delete your account anytime. Contact info@koffykraft.com for account deletion requests.

Third Parties

Mailgun: Receives your email for sign-in codes only.
Cloudflare: Hosts the app and database (stores your roast data).
GitHub: Hosts our code (your data stays private in D1).

Your data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of KoffyKraft means you accept the changes.
