Plots

Save and share

Files carry each plot's outline, name, variety, area and height, and each spot's label. Attach them to a crop record, a sale, or a green lot so the roaster and the brewer can see where the coffee grew.
Heights come from free satellite terrain data (SRTM, about 30 m grid, through the Terrain Tiles open dataset) or your phone's GPS. Both can be off by 10 m or more: good for the lie of the land and the height of an estate, not for a single terrace step. If you know a surveyed height, type it on the plot in Origin and it is used first. Map imagery: Esri World Imagery and OpenStreetMap contributors. Place search: OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
